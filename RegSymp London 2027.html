Why Now.

About RegSymp

The financial services sector is at an inflection point. Open Banking is moving from policy paper to operational reality. The retail payments oversight regime is live. AML, sanctions and client-asset frameworks are tightening and the boards of every major institution are being asked harder questions than ever before.

RegSymp brings together CEOs, Founders, CCOs, CROs, MLROs, COOs, General Counsels and Heads of Custody from the leading banks, asset managers, dealers, fintechs and crypto firms alongside their regulators, auditors and advisors to debate what comes next.

The Programme Thesis

Five critical themes
for 2027.

Every session at RegSymp maps to one of five themes that the programme committee has identified as the most consequential regulatory and operational shifts facing major financial institutions over the next 24 months.

  1. 01

    The Future of European Finance

    Can Europe lead the next era of financial innovation across banking, payments, AI and digital assets while remaining globally competitive?

  2. 02

    Payments, Stablecoins & Programmable Money

    How embedded finance, stablecoins, tokenisation and next-generation payment infrastructure are reshaping the movement of money.

  3. 03

    AI, Wealth & The Reinvention of Banking

    Exploring whether AI will transform — or replace — traditional banking, wealth management and private banking models.

  4. 04

    Trust, Infrastructure & Institutional Resilience

    The operational backbone behind modern finance: custody, safeguarding, operational resilience, cybersecurity and scalable infrastructure.

  5. 05

    The New Financial Ecosystem

    How banks, fintechs, family offices, digital asset firms, investors and technology leaders are converging to build the next generation of financial services.

Who Should Attend

A room of peers.

RegSymp is designed for the people who actually own the regulatory outcomes inside major financial institutions.

CEOs, Founders, CROs, COOs, Chief Compliance Officers, MLROs, General Counsel, Heads of Custody, Heads of Operational Resilience, Internal Audit Partners, Regulatory Advisors and Big 4 Partners.

Practitioner-First. No Vendor Presentations on the Main Stage.

Confirmed Speakers.

RegSymp's speaker faculty is drawn exclusively from sitting Heads of Function at major international financial institutions, the regulators who oversee them, and the senior advisors closest to the work. Every speaker is in the room for the full two days.
