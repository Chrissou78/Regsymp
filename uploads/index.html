About RegSymp

The financial services sector is at an inflection point. Open Banking is moving from policy paper to operational reality. The retail payments oversight regime is live. AML, sanctions and client-asset frameworks are tightening and the boards of every major institution are being asked harder questions than ever before.

RegSymp brings together CCOs, CROs, MLROs, COOs, General Counsel and Heads of Custody from the leading banks, asset managers, dealers, fintechs and crypto firms alongside their regulators, auditors and advisors to debate what comes next.

The Programme Thesis

Five critical themes
for 2026.

Every session at RegSymp maps to one of five themes that the programme committee has identified as the most consequential regulatory and operational shifts facing major financial institutions over the next 24 months.

01

Financial Crime, AML & Fraud

The new FINTRAC supervisory model, sanctions screening at the edge, deepfake-enabled fraud, and the operationalisation of effectiveness over technical compliance.

02

Crypto, Tokenisation & Payments

VASP licensing pathways, stablecoin frameworks, tokenised deposits, and how the retail payments oversight regime reshapes who is — and isn't — a payment service provider.

03

Client Assets, Custody & Safeguarding

Segregation, reconciliation, and the operational realities of safeguarding client money and assets across multi-entity, multi-jurisdiction structures.

04

Operational Resilience & TPRM

Important business services, impact tolerances, third-party concentration risk, and what a credible recovery test actually looks like under OSFI Guideline B-10.

05

Technology, AI & Model Risk

Model governance for generative AI, the evolving expectations on explainability, and how compliance functions are themselves being re-tooled by the technology they oversee.

A privately convened dinner.
Twenty-five guests. Chatham House rules.

Tables are pre-assigned to maximise cross-function exposure. Every course arrives with a structured prompt. Every conversation has a chair. Nothing is recorded. Nothing is pitched.

The Team Behind RegSymp

About the Organiser.

RegSymp is an independent symposium series founded by practitioners, for practitioners. We are not a media company, not a consultancy, and not a technology vendor. We exist because the conversations that matter most in regulatory leadership are not happening in the right rooms.

The series is governed by a small programme committee of sitting Heads of Function who set the agenda, vet the speaker faculty, and chair the sessions themselves. The organising team handles logistics, delegate relations, and sponsorship everything else is practitioner-led.

Contact the Team.

Delegate desk, sponsorship enquiries, and speaker submissions are all handled by the same small team. We respond within one business day.
