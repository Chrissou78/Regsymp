The financial services sector is at an inflection point. Open Banking is moving from policy paper to operational reality. The retail payments oversight regime is live.

The Programme Thesis

Five critical themes for 2026.

Every session maps to one of five themes the programme committee has identified as most consequential for major financial institutions over the next 24 months.

01

Financial Crime, AML & Fraud

FINTRAC supervisory model, sanctions screening at the edge, deepfake-enabled fraud.

02

Crypto, Tokenisation & Payments

VASP licensing, stablecoin frameworks, tokenised deposits and the payments perimeter.

03

Client Assets, Custody & Safeguarding

Segregation, reconciliation, multi-jurisdiction structures and the audit trail.

04

Operational Resilience & TPRM

Impact tolerances, third-party concentration risk, credible recovery testing.

05

Technology, AI & Model Risk

Generative AI governance, explainability expectations and compliance re-tooling.
