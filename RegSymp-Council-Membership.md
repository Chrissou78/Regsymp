# REGSYMP COUNCIL

A year-round membership community for senior leaders across financial services, regulation, payments and digital assets.

The RegSymp Council extends the conversation beyond the symposium — providing members with ongoing access, peer connections and insight through a curated network of professionals and organisations worldwide.

All memberships are annual.

---

## COUNCIL MEMBER
**£1,250 per year**

For senior professionals, consultants, lawyers, risk and compliance leaders, fintech founders and executives.

**Membership Benefits:**
- Quarterly RegSymp Council Briefing
- Personal profile within the RegSymp Council Member Directory
- Inclusion in member communications and updates
- Access to the Council network
- Opportunity to contribute ideas and topics for future Council publications
- Founding Member status — limited to the first 50 Founding Members worldwide

---

---

## IMPORTANT

RegSymp Council membership is entirely separate from RegSymp conferences, sponsorship packages, speaking opportunities and invitation-only events.

Membership does not include conference tickets, sponsorship rights, speaking engagements or preferential access to RegSymp events.

The Council exists for a different purpose: year-round engagement, thought leadership and professional connection between symposia.
